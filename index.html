Prototype methodology

Nash / Shift model notes

This interactive prototype uses illustrative modeled values alongside Metro planning context. It is not investment advice and should not be treated as a live property valuation or property-listing tool.

  • Opportunity score blends market upside, affordability, jobs, mobility, permits, taxes, displacement, flood exposure, and heat exposure.
  • Address-level opportunity nodes are analytical starting points, not verified listings or claims that a parcel is available.
  • Neighborhood boundaries are loaded from the public Google My Maps reference supplied by the user; surrounding counties use the U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb county layer.
